Narsil

A digital museum in your pocket

Narsil is an art discovery app. Browse public-domain masterpieces and original works, read the history, follow artists, and save what you love. This page is public — no account is required to read it.

What Narsil does

  • Scroll an endless feed of museum art and freelance originals.
  • Learn artists, eras, and places with quizzes and history notes.
  • Save, like, and follow across devices when you create an account.
  • Optional canvas-print orders and Narsil Pro subscriptions.

Accounts and Google sign-in

You can browse as a guest. If you choose Continue with Google, Narsil receives your Google name, email address, and public profile so we can create or sign in to your account. We do not post to Google on your behalf. Details are in our Privacy Policy.
