Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Narsil (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you use the Narsil mobile and web application, including when you sign in with Google.

Contact: narsil.business@gmail.com

1. Information we collect

Google sign-in. If you tap Continue with Google, Google authenticates you and shares with us:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your public Google profile information (such as profile photo, if provided)

We request only the basic OpenID / email / profile scopes needed to create an account. We do not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or the ability to send email as you.

Account information. Email, display name, avatar, role, and settings are stored with our authentication and database provider (Supabase) so we can keep you signed in and apply security rules to your data.

App activity. Saved artworks, likes, follows, seen items, quiz progress, terms acceptance, and similar in-app choices tied to your account.

Support messages. If you contact us in-app or by email, we receive the content of that conversation and your account email.

Purchases and subscriptions. Canvas-print checkout and Narsil Pro payments are processed by Stripe and/or Apple. We receive order status, product, and entitlement information. We do not store your full card number.

Device and technical data. Standard connection data such as app version and crash/error logs may be recorded so we can operate and debug the service.

2. How we use information

  • Create and authenticate your Narsil account
  • Sync saved art, likes, follows, and quiz progress across devices
  • Provide customer support
  • Process orders and subscriptions you request
  • Protect the service (fraud, abuse, security)
  • Improve Narsil features

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Sharing

We share information with service providers who help us run Narsil:

  • Google — sign-in (see Google Privacy Policy)
  • Supabase — authentication and database hosting
  • Stripe and Apple — payments and App Store subscriptions
  • Hosting and content-delivery providers for the app and images

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect Narsil, our users, or the public.

4. Retention

We keep account and activity data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymize personal data we control except where we must keep records (for example, orders or legal obligations).

5. Your choices

  • You may use Narsil as a guest without Google sign-in.
  • You may disconnect Google sign-in by deleting your Narsil account.
  • To access, correct, or delete your data, email narsil.business@gmail.com.

6. Children

Narsil is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Security

We use industry-standard protections offered by our providers (encrypted transport, access-controlled databases). No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

8. International processing

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. By using Narsil you understand your information may be transferred to those locations.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above will change when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
